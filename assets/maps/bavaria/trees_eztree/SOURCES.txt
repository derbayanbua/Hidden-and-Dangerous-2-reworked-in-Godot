Bäume / Trees – Bavaria (trees_eztree)

DE:
- Geometrie (ez_*.glb): erzeugt mit dem Baumgenerator EZ-Tree (https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree),
  MIT-Lizenz, Copyright (c) 2024 Daniel Greenheck. Erzeugt per Node-Skript (Tree.createGeometry, drei LOD-Stufen),
  eigene Parameter je Art (Fichte, Buche, Eiche, Birke, Obstbaum), Varianten s/m/l. Dreiecke: triangles.txt.
- Laubkarten (leaves_*.png): aus den Laubtexturen des EZ-Tree-Repositorys (MIT, s. o.), umgefärbt/aufbereitet.
- Rinde Fichte/Eiche/Buche/Obstbaum (bark_*): ambientCG Bark015/Bark001/Bark006/Bark004 (CC0 1.0), umgefärbt.
- Rinde Birke (bark_birch_*): prozedural im Projekt erzeugt.

EN:
- Geometry (ez_*.glb): generated with the EZ-Tree tree generator (https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree),
  MIT License, Copyright (c) 2024 Daniel Greenheck. Generated by a Node script (Tree.createGeometry, three LOD levels),
  own parameters per species (spruce, beech, oak, birch, fruit tree), variants s/m/l. Triangles: triangles.txt.
- Leaf cards (leaves_*.png): from the EZ-Tree repository leaf textures (MIT, see above), recoloured/processed.
- Bark spruce/oak/beech/fruit (bark_*): ambientCG Bark015/Bark001/Bark006/Bark004 (CC0 1.0), recoloured.
- Bark birch (bark_birch_*): generated procedurally in this project.
